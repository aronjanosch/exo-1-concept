# Decisions — EXO-1 (working title)

Status: living document, last updated 2026-10-10. Only what the initiator decided, quoted in their words with a date (see `AGENTS.md`). Sections are by topic; inside a section the rows are not in date order, every row carries its own date. Rows that a later decision replaced are under "Superseded". Details and sources: `FEASIBILITY.md`, `CORE-LOOP.md`, `ROADMAP.md`.

### World and planets

| Topic | Decision |
|---|---|
| World | Fixed hand-built system. Small but complete, seamless planets. Procedural terrain, hand-built city and outposts |
| Planet size | Radius 5 km as a first guide value. Larger and smaller planets are possible |
| Planet scale (direction) | The standard planet grows well beyond Hearth's 5 km; the size is chosen for flight, since flight is the foundation (row "Flight is the foundation"): low flight needs mountains far below the sky ceiling, approach and landing need distance. Measured in what players feel (flight minutes between places, the climb to space), with ceilings first: the tallest terrain about 1/10 of the atmosphere. Proposal: SC-moon class, radius 150-300 km, atmosphere 30-40 km, mountains 3-5 km. The spike runs 30, 100 and 300 km (code repo #177); the number is decided after flying it. Emptiness is answered by worldgen: places by density rules and a few large set pieces, like Minecraft's villages and modded terrain (`research/worldgen-and-lod.md`). Initiator, 2026-10-09: "Ja 15 ist aber etwas wenig wenn man das mit allem andern Vergleicht." Initiator, 2026-10-10: "Die tiefe des fliegens auch wie landeplätze hailen usw macht kein sinn wenn alles so nah ist. Und auch das fliegen in der Athmosphäre ist komisch wenn berge im prinzip ins weltall ragen müssen" and "Mit der neuen Tech sollte ja ein SC nicht umbedingt unmöglich sein. [...] Aber selbst so große welten lassen sich mit guter Worldgen beleben, siehe Minecraft." |
| Planet terrain architecture (direction) | Like Star Citizen: the seed goes over the network, never terrain; every client generates locally around itself, so memory follows the camera and not the planet. A coarse global layer (compact, u16 heights and u8 masks, about 100 m cells at 30-60 km, about 1 km at Star Citizen size) is baked once per client and cached on disk under a hash of recipe, seed and code version; drainage runs on it and outputs rivers as polylines. Fine detail (noise, landform stamps as functions, river carve, place edits) is generated per quadtree chunk. Heights stay on the CPU, one code path for rendering and collision; the GPU only for visual extras if a measurement asks for it. Client authority stays: the host relays and needs terrain only where it simulates unowned things; the ownership rule for crates and enemies comes with H. Replaces the fine global bake. Spike: code repo #177. Initiator, 2026-10-09: "wieso nicht so wie in SC? das würde den host total entlasten" and "Ja unsere Grafik ist einfach also ich bin offen für deine Experten Meinung". Small planets and moons use the same path; the fine global bake is not kept as a second path. LOD: the per-face quadtree with skirts. Initiator, 2026-10-10: "NMS und SC haben doch schon gezeigt dass Seed basiert der weg ist. [...] Wieso am alten festhalten was gibt es uns für vorteile wenn nicht nur limitierungen. Kleine Planeten könnten auch via Seed generiert werden, oder?" |
| World values and scale | No global scale constant. Each planet carries its radii (obstruction, arrival, frame zone) in its content file as absolute metres, like Star Citizen's per-body records, every value set explicitly, nothing derived from the radius (initiator, 2026-10-08: "macht das star citizen auch mit den derived from planet radius, wenn nicht lassen wir das auch erstmal weg. simplizität"; the SC records set every value per body). System values (distances between planets, quantum drive speed and acceleration) sit in one place in metres, so a later change of the standard planet size is one change there. Atmosphere and terrain stay per planet recipe. Initiator, 2026-10-08, after being asked whether to build a scale constant or keep it simple: "ja lass uns das auch machen". Source: `research/quantum-drive-reference.md` |
| Planet is a shell | The planet stays a height shell you walk on; no digging, no voxel terrain. Initiator, 2026-10-08: "Hülle". Answers the fork in `research/procedural-planet.md`, section 10. Dungeons are instanced interiors; caves inside the shell (mesh under a hole in the terrain) stay possible, the shell must not rule them out (backlog #75). Initiator, 2026-10-09: "finde dungeons als instanzen super. da können wir uns dann richtig austoben. aber wir sollten uns nicht verbauen auch höhlen einzubauen, ist interessant zu erkunden" |
| Planets are static | No rotation, no orbit; time of day through the sun direction; the frame model keeps rotation possible. Same approval |
| Planets in spike 11 | Two practically identical planets (same radius and atmosphere, different seed). Different radii and atmospheres are a separate feature later. Initiator, 2026-10-08: "Ja gerne andere Radien und andere Atmosphären. aber das ist ein anderes Feature würde ich sagen. Für den Spike einfach 2 planeten nehmen die quasi identisch sind." |
| Interiors | Small shops stay in the open world, large or complex interiors (for example a sewer) are instanced |
| Scale | City is small, dense and simple, like Schedule I |
| Movement on planets | Walking anywhere is possible; the player decides whether to walk or take the ship. Natural obstacles (steep slopes, water) may stop a walker, no path around them is guaranteed. Initiator, 2026-10-07: "die möglichkeit gibts auch zu Füß muss er selbst wissen ob er das machen will. warum sollte er das nicht amchen können? wenn es natürliche hindernisse gibt dann kann er halt nicht weiter". Source: `SPIKE-8-REPORT.md` |
| Places (hand-placed) | Hand-built places (pads now, the city and outposts later) sit at a fixed spot on a planet; the procedural terrain stays. One JSON file per place in `content/place/`: planet, latitude and longitude, a turn, the ground edits under it (the same flatten, smooth and raise edits as the site kinds; a pad flattens a disc, buildings at the edge may follow the terrain), and its pads in place-local metres (x, y as in `art/city/city_plan.py`). `planet_core` reads the places when it bakes; generated sites keep away from them. A location names a place and a pad in it, so the city becomes one place with many pads and locations. Until models exist a pad shows as a plain glowing ring; the city's models come from `art/city` (its anchors for doors, pads and npcs). Initiator, 2026-10-09: "Wir wollen ja noch eine Stadt auf den planeten setzen. Also handcrafted objekte die dann zum teil auf ebenen sind oder sich dem terrain anpassen. lass uns das hier am besten auch machen oder?"; chosen by the agent after reading the city branch, on the initiator's "guck mal in den branch rein und dann beantworte selbst". Research: `research/place-authoring.md` |

### Movement, flight and HUD

| Topic | Decision |
|---|---|
| Flight is the foundation | Flight is the centre of the game's fiction and fantasy, not a means to get somewhere (unlike the skateboard in Schedule I). The game is flight plus production plus planning and automation plus progression; on-foot shooting comes on top. Planet scale follows from it (row "Planet scale"). Initiator, 2026-10-10: "Wenn fliegen Spaß macht dann gelingt das Projekt. Fliegen plus Produktion plus PLanen und automatisieren und Progresssion. On top kommt dann FPS zeug." and "hier ist es aber Zentrale Teil der FIktion und der Fantasy" |
| Flight model | One flight model: the SC model (`flight_core::sc`), as close to Star Citizen's flight as our research reaches; the axis model and the assist switch are gone (code repo #206). Switches as in SC, with SC's words: C coupled/decoupled (blends over 4 s), H gravity compensation (off: the ship falls), F8 G-safe, B master mode SCM/NAV, U comstab, P proximity assist, I wind compensation, Page Up/Down speed limiter, K landing mode. X brakes along the velocity (the heading of travel holds), the speed cap refuses thrust along the velocity, Shift boosts from a capacitor (about 20 s, refills in about 27 s). Thrust is force on mass with spool per thruster group and jerk; rotation is a spring with overshoot; air gives drag per axis, lift, weathervane, wind and ground turbulence. Values are derived from a small Star Citizen fighter's flight records and adapted (times, rates and ratios taken over, speeds scaled to our 150 m/s SCM cap, our own mass, nothing copied 1:1, see Inspiration); tuned by feel. Open: #202-#204, ship profiles. Initiator, 2026-10-10: "gib mir einfach das neue model das sich mit einer taste aktiviert mit so viel von SC drin wie möglich", "nimm die werte aus sc und passe sie an unser projekt an", "das richtige SC modell das neuste als default", "Altes Flugmodell kann raus" and "Wir bleiben bei dem SC modell nicht wieder Assistant. das war doch der ganze Sinn hier". Code repo #194, #205, #206 |
| Movement in space | A walker outside a ship in zero gravity keeps its velocity (it drifts with the ship it left) and moves with suit thrusters, same keys as the ship (WASD, Space/Ctrl, Q/E roll, Shift boost, X brake to rest), free orientation in all axes. Initiator, 2026-10-07: "der walker soll sich im weltraum dann auch irgendwann mit ausrüstung bewegen können." The suit works only in zero gravity for now; initiator, 2026-10-07: "Ne wir machen einfach erst in schwerelosigkeit." Starting values (thrust 2 m/s², boost x3, brake 4 m/s², roll 1.5 rad/s, no fuel), initiator: "die werte passen erstmal". Source: issues #5, #8 |
| Walking feel | A short step-off: 0 to 3 m/s within 0.1 s, then full speed at once; stopping below 3 m/s eases over 0.1 s. So a tap of W is a slow step and a player can step out of a ship carefully. Initiator, 2026-10-07: "der spieler braucht eine kurze beschleunigung und tapping von w heißt dass man langsam raus gehen würde. weil sont hat man immer diese 5m/s und das ist ein harter übergang", after trying 15 m/s²: "die beschleunigung ist viel zu lahm und auch nur von 0 auf 5 sollte das passieren oder von u-3 innerhalb von 0.1 sekunden und dann voll. damit tapping geht mehr nicht". Entering or leaving a cabin keeps the look direction. Leaving a cabin keeps the camera orientation (no step); in gravity the walker then rights itself slowly "like a slanted fall", weightless it stays as it is. Initiator, 2026-10-08: "beim austeigen behalten wir die orientierung der kamera bei so dass es keinen sprung gibt. In gravitation bleibt richtet man sich dann langsam auf wie bei einem schrägen fall. bei schwerelosigkeit bleibt man in der orientierung." Righting: time constant 0.5 s, at most 90 deg/s (start values). Source: issue #7 |
| Cabin gravity (LAG) | Ships have localized artificial gravity in the cabin. **Off while landed** (a tilted landed ship is a slope under planet gravity, so walking in and out does not turn the view), comes up over about 1 s after take-off and goes down after landing; in flight it is always on, also upside down. G switches it on by hand in a landed ship. Initiator, 2026-10-08: "wir könnten auch die schiff schwerkraft in einem gelandeten schiff ausmachen und erst beim abheben einschalten", "die akutelle variante von uns ist nicht gut" (gravity turning over the ramp), on the plan: "LAG kann man manuell anschalten? Dann ja gelandetes schiff hat LAG aus. Später packen wir ja noch mehr features dazu so wie schiff ann und aus machen" and "finde deinen plan gut". Ship power and other ship systems come later. Star Citizen research (LAG, physics grids): see the session notes in the PR |
| Flight feel plan | Flight is lifted towards Star Citizen in our low-fidelity look; plan in code repo epic #143, research in `research/flight-feel.md`. Every feel effect works in the chase view too; head motion from G comes later. Initiator, 2026-10-09: "Lass uns das Fliegen nun wirklich auf ein nächstes Level heben, a la Star Citizen", on the plan: "Lass mal den Kopf weg. Das kommt später. muss auch in der außen ansicht klappen. Rest gehe ich mit." |
| Ship model and ship structure | Our own ship with canopy and cockpit, as a Blender script, made by a designer session (not by the flight lane). Ships get controls you operate by hand and may consist of several walkable sections, as in Star Citizen. Initiator, 2026-10-09: "Du schreibst keinen Design biref das macht dann ein anderer. Ich will nämlich auch bedien elemnete im schiff und schiffe aus mehreren Begehbaren Teilen wie in SC auch". Issues #159, #160, #161 |
| Ships as their own sprints | Ships get one or more feature sprints of their own: switching systems on and off, cockpit displays you operate, upgrade paths and reasons to upgrade, space battles, pirates, heavy jobs a small ship cannot do. The loop takes only a few basic mechanics from that now, seen from the jobs' side. Initiator, 2026-10-09: "schiffe brauchen ihren eigenen kompletten Feature Sprint bzw mehrere. bedienung wie an und aus machen cockpit displays die sich bedienen lassen, upgrade pfade, dann aber auch gründe warum man upgraded, space battles, piraten, Schwere aufträge die sich nicht so einfach mit einem kleinen schiff lösen lassen." |
| Brake | X brakes in every mode along the actual velocity, so the heading of travel holds, with the boost's thrust like Star Citizen's spacebrake ("spacebrake engages boost"); the weakest axis that way sets the rate. Initiator, 2026-10-08: "passt"; along the velocity since round 5 (row "Flight model"). Spec: issue #24 |
| Mouse flight | The ship follows the mouse as a virtual joystick by default: the cursor offset is an angle with a dead zone and a maximum, the ship turns at deflection times its turn rate, the HUD shows marker, line and dead-zone circle. Today's direct mode stays as an option in the bindings. A mouse-aim mode stays out. The camera leans into turns (look-ahead). Initiator, 2026-10-08, on this proposal after the Star Citizen HUD records were read: "okay" |
| vjoy details | Range in degrees per axis (start 15°), a light dead zone of 5 % of the range, no recentre key, as in Star Citizen. Initiator, 2026-10-08: "wie bei sc, und es braucht eine leichte deadzone um die mitte". Look-ahead as in Star Citizen, simplified to one point: the camera leans towards the vjoy cursor with a player-set strength. Initiator: "wie sc". Spec: issues #24, #27 |
| HUD | At most four permanent elements (mode, speed, altitude near a planet, boost); brake, LAG, warp and gravity-off only when relevant; debug lines behind F3. Initiator, 2026-10-08: "ja". Spec: issue #24. In the seat a flight panel adds the switches as badges, a toast on each switch, and flight gauges (speed against the cap, G, thrust, velocity vector, the horizon only in the atmosphere), round 5 (#197, #200). Initiator, 2026-10-10: "Auch das Hud kannst du anpassen." |
| Ship tuning | Not planned and out of scope. May come later. The Gummi-Ship reference is dropped entirely |
| Travel time | "No more than about 30 s without input or an event" (`CORE-LOOP.md`) is a guide value for the start, never a hard limit. Initiator, 2026-10-08: "das harte limit mit 30 skunden bitte als hartes limit entferne [...] das soll ein richtert für den Anfang sein. Ja mit 30 sek weiter machen, aber wenn es 45 oder mal 55 dauert ist auch okay." |
| Quantum drive: where it may start | Only above 1.5 times the planet's atmosphere height (per planet, so larger planets with a higher atmosphere get a higher limit). Initiator, 2026-10-08: "sagen wir 1,5x der atmosphäre. so können wir je nach größe des planetes gehen. große planeten haben auch eine höhere athmosphäre." |
| Quantum drive: emergency exit | The pilot can leave a running warp early, as in Star Citizen. Initiator, 2026-10-08: "So wie bei Star Citizen, kann man das doch auch oder? Bitte übernehemen". Star Citizen's records show only the status message (`ship_quantumdriveemergencyexit`), no tuning values; the behaviour is ours to define |
| Quantum drive: arrival | The ship arrives heading straight at the target planet, looking at its centre, at a good distance outside the atmosphere with the planet clearly in front. Initiator, 2026-10-08: "Man soll direkt auf den planeten zufliegen so dass man im prinzip auf seinen mittelpunkt oder kern guckt mit einem guten abstand von der athmosphäre weg aber den plaeten deutlich vor sich hat" |
| Quantum drive: arrival side | The arrival point follows the path, not the sun; arriving on the night side is fine. Initiator, 2026-10-08: "nein, wenn das die Nacht seite ist dann ist das so. fühlt sich echter an wenn es so ist" |
| Quantum drive: exit speed | The ship leaves the warp at 400 m/s (`exit_speed` in `system.json`); after the exit the normal flight control owns the speed (flight assist brakes, without it the ship flies on). Star Citizen's records set no exit speed, its tutorial says the same about flight control after the exit (`research/quantum-drive-reference.md`). Initiator, 2026-10-08: "ja lassen wir" |

### Cargo and the loop (milestones C and D)

| Topic | Decision |
|---|---|
| Cargo is physical | Crates in a few standard sizes, carried by hand, limit by volume; no counter. Same approval as above |
| Grab and cargo (milestone C) | (1) A crate in a cabin lives in the ship's frame like the walker and is held to the ship during warp. (2) Holding is physical: a spring to a hold point with a force cap, heavy crates lag, turning is damped on contact. (3) A small crate takes one hand; a bigger one takes two hands, which means slower walking, no sprint and no jump. (4) Crate sizes are a data table with sizes that double; for milestone C there are three: small (one hand), medium (two hands), large (two players); later machines move the large ones and bigger sizes. (5) Network (#36): whoever holds a crate owns it; after release the last toucher simulates it until it rests, then the ship's owner (in a cabin) or the host (outside) takes it; in a shared carry the first grabber owns it and the second adds force. (6) A crate set down on a floor grid in the cabin is mag-locked; only unlocked crates slide in flight. (7) Throw: release with the hand's velocity plus a throw button with a fixed impulse, less for heavy crates. (8) Hands to about 2 m plus a hand tool, full force to about 6 m and falling off to about 10 m, that can also turn the crate; values are starting points for the playtest. Initiator, 2026-10-09, on the questions in `research/grab-and-cargo.md`: "1. b 2. a 3 a 4. erstmal ja und die größte kann auch via maschinen (später dann verschoben werden) 5. a 6. b. 7. b"; on 8 after a check against Star Citizen: "Ja machen wir so"; on the size table: "passt" |
| No combat before the loop | No weapons or combat before the loop playtest (milestone D); the grab tool is the first tool. Initiator, 2026-10-08: "Gunplay und Kampf muss sich erst gut anfühlen und dann kommt Content." Later vision: dungeons with good gunplay like Destiny, loot and jobs, even hostile planets with enemy fortifications |
| Loop D: provisional | The milestone D decisions below are a starting point to be changed by the loop playtest. Initiator, 2026-10-09, on the summary of the grilling: "ja stimmt, machen wir erstmal so und ändern wir durch playtest". Options and sources: `research/loop-references.md` section 5. Spec: epic #37 in the code repo |
| Job model | A job is a template with a list of objectives. Each objective is a pure reducer over domain events (crate picked up, crate delivered with place and condition, place reached, later enemy defeated, item collected …) with a typed condition from the data; reward, deadline, failure and unlocks sit once on the job. The host applies the events; event ids make duplicates harmless. Milestone D builds only the objective *deliver* (legs A→B with two or three modifiers); further objective kinds (bounty, find, collect drops, harvest or ore deliveries) come with the system that needs them; chains through "start follow-up job". Lives in `jobs_core` (see "Gameplay crates and build order (D)"). Initiator, 2026-10-09: "Q1 nochmal neu diskutieren, lass uns eine moderne und robuste methode wählen." Chosen by the agent on the initiator's delegation: "Du entscheidest was am besten passt". The broader vision (bounty jobs, story, finding a crate, mob drops, farming like Schedule I, asteroid mining, ship types per job) stays the target; initiator, 2026-10-09: "War die idee einen ersten Core loop zu machen und später die systeme auszubauen" |
| Money (loop D) | A fixed reward per job, graded by delivered share, condition and time; no market in D. Every commodity carries a base price already, so trade and sales from later production need no rebuild. The only money sink in D is buying unlocks; running costs (fuel and so on) not before the playtest. Initiator, 2026-10-09: "passt" |
| Progression | Two layers. Crew layer (shared, in the host save): wallet, unlocked places and job boards, later base and production. Personal layer (per character): career tracks per job kind, later own ships, gear, cosmetics. Money, XP and reputation are all *progress tracks* (owner crew or player, value, thresholds); unlocks and jobs refer to tracks in their conditions. A level limits only what the accepting player may take; others can always join; money goes to the crew wallet, XP to each participant. D: crew unlocks bought with money; one personal track "freight XP" is counted and shown but gates nothing. Reputation stays parked. Initiator, 2026-10-09: "Denke gemeinsame Progressoin aber jder hat auch gewisse freiheitsgrade" and on the proposal "Ja gerne erstmal so" |
| Production (loop D) | No production in milestone D. The *deliver* objective asks for commodity and amount, not a specific crate, so farming and mining can feed it later; each gets its own milestone after the loop playtest. Initiator, 2026-10-09: "A". Answers the open question in `CORE-LOOP.md` |
| Loop content schema | Templates plus pools: a job template names conditions instead of fixed places (pickup with tag X, dropoff with tag Y, not the same place, commodity from pool P); a seeded generator draws concrete offers; texts from fragment pools. Targeted and story jobs use the same schema with a fixed place and commodity, *once only* and a follow-up job. Records for D: `commodity`, `site`, `job_template`, `unlock`, `progress_track`, `encounter`; unlocks are records of their own, the unlocked thing carries a tag. The loader validates schema plus cross references and shares its code with the tuning loader. Initiator, 2026-10-09: "B, mit der OPtion auch für Story oder so gezielte Aufträge zu machen" |
| Encounters (route events) | Called *encounters* from 2026-10-09 so that "event" only means a domain event. Hazards and upsides are shown on the job up front (at most one warning and one upside) with a hazard bonus. A thin base pool of harmless events runs on every flight; the job's modifiers raise frequency and danger. Timer with cooldown per template and a seed, no intensity director; an event is a record with trigger condition, weight, cooldown and an effect that emits the same domain events as jobs. No combat events in D. Initiator, 2026-10-09: "C", and on the follow-up "ii" |

## Open

- **Name:** "EXO-1" cannot stay. A game named "Exo One" (developer Exbleative, Steam and consoles, since 2021) already exists in the same field. Official trademark registers (EUIPO/TMview, DPMA, USPTO) were not checked. Until a new name is chosen, "EXO-1" is only a working title. Do not use it in videos, thumbnails, domains or store pages. The docs and repo names still use it for now.
- Where votes run. Proposal from research: discussion on GitHub, Discord only for polls.
- Details of the content schema (initiator wants to review). The loop records are settled in "Loop content schema" (2026-10-09); the exact fields follow in the milestone D spec.
- AgentBridge interface (research proposal: `get_state`, `do_action`, `step`, `reset`, no `eval`; see `FEASIBILITY.md`).
- Travel between planets: direction like No Man's Sky or Star Citizen, to be tried.
- Contact authority between ships: ships should affect each other physically (initiator, 2026-10-06), but with client authority each side computes a contact alone and the two histories can disagree (spike 4 fixture: 217 ms and 3 m apart). Candidates: pair rule (one fixed side computes the contact for both), migrating authority for touching/docked groups, damage-only ramming where each owner decides its own damage.
